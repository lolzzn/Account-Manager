This Account Manager Doesn't contain any malware. If your antivirus is detecting it has virus, it's a false-positive because the rMultiThreader is compiled.



HOW DOES IT WORK?
	This account manager works by creating a chrome/opera profile in the file named "ChromeProfileSave". It's ran completely on your device, so it's safe to use.

HOW DOES THE "JOIN GAME" WORKS AND WHY SOMETIMES ITS MAKING THE APPLICATION FREEZE?
	The "Join Game" freeze when you load up a new game to the software. Why? Because it's trying to get the game ID of that specific roblox game and see if its valid or not.
	
	IS THIS SAFE?
	Yes, it's safe to use and doesn't contain any malware. Like i said earlier, it's ran completely on your device.
	
	IS THERE A LIMIT ON HOW MANY ACCOUNT I CAN PUT?
	I have no idea, to my knowledge its based off your computer storage. I have only tried ~30 account and it works fine. Pretty sure it can go up to 1000, I'm not sure
	
	
HOW TO USE IT:
	
	LOGIN:
		Step 1:
			Enter a valid username (no special characters) and press "Create Profile"
		
		Step 2:
			Login to your roblox account via the browser that it just opened.
	
	JOIN GAME:
		Step 1:
			Select your profile, enter a VALID game id, and finnaly click the join game button (might take a while to load)
			
		Step 2:
			Then the game you just put there is saved on the "game list" and you can launch it anytime without delay.
		
		Sub, Delete Game:
			This will just remove the game from your "game list" tab.
			
	CLEAR CACHE: (Recommended to do it each time you made a profile)
		Step 1:
			Press it.
		
		Step 2:
			Wait.
			
		Step 3:
			Done.
